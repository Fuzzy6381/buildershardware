Quality Building Materials & Hardware

Builders Hardware is your local hardware destination in Bayview, Chatsworth.

Builders Hardware is a local hardware supplier serving the Bayview and Chatsworth community. We offer a wide selection of building materials, tools, plumbing supplies, electrical products and general hardware for construction, maintenance and home improvement.

Whether you are working on a small home improvement project or a larger building job, we aim to make finding the right products simple and convenient.
